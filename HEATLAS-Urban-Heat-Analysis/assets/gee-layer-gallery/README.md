# GEE Layer Gallery

This gallery contains three selected visual references from the Google Earth Engine (GEE) implementation for the Jaipur pilot of **HEATLAS — Urban Heat Intelligence and Mitigation Platform**.

The figures are intended to give readers a quick visual understanding of the working GEE environment, the heat-analysis outputs and the mitigation-suitability mapping. They are representative examples, not a screenshot of every layer generated by the script. All images are captured from the actual HEATLAS script running in the GEE Code Editor.

## 1. GEE workspace and Jaipur study grid

Show the GEE Code Editor with `Jaipur_Heat_v3.js` open, the Jaipur study area visible on the map, and the Layers panel expanded. Where possible, make the Jaipur boundary and 1 km grid visible. This figure establishes the study area, spatial framework and working analysis environment.

## 3. Mitigation-suitability mapping

Show one representative mitigation-suitability map, for example `Cool Roof Zones`, with its legend or map values visible where available. Expand the Layers panel to show the four rule-based suitability layers: `Cool Roof Zones`, `Urban Forest Zones`, `Water Cooling Zones` and `Reflective Surface Zones`.

These are screening masks based on the thresholds encoded in the script. They identify cells that meet the respective conditions; they are not field-validated intervention plans and do not estimate cooling benefits or implementation feasibility.

## Other layers in the analysis

The script also displays spectral and environmental indicators, including NDVI, NDBI, NDWI, albedo, FVC, built density, aerosol, terrain, land cover, evapotranspiration, LAI, soil moisture and population-related layers. Composite outputs include Heat Storage, Cooling Capacity, Exposure, Vulnerability and Resilience. These are documented in the [GEE implementation README](../../GEE/README.md); they are not all shown as separate gallery images.
